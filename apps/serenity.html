App guide

Serenity

Serenity is designed for quiet focus, recovery, and short wellness moments from your watch.

Basic use

  • Open the app when you want to start a calm session.
  • Choose the available duration or mode if the app provides options.
  • Follow the on-screen timing, prompts, or session indicators.
  • End the session when finished or let the timer complete.

Troubleshooting

  • Make sure notifications and focus settings on your device are not blocking expected alerts.
  • Restart the app if a timer or session does not update.
  • Contact support with the device model and app version.

Privacy

Serenity is designed to work without advertising or tracking. See the privacy policy.
