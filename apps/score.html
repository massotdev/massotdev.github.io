App guide

Score

Score is a simple watch companion for tracking points during matches, games, or training drills.

Basic use

  • Open the app before starting your activity.
  • Use the plus controls to update each side or player.
  • Use reset when you start a new match or drill.
  • Keep the app open while you need the score visible.

Troubleshooting

  • Restart the app if controls stop responding.
  • Check your watch storage and system version if the app does not open.
  • Contact support with the device model and a short description of the issue.

Privacy

Score is designed to work without advertising or tracking. See the privacy policy.
