App guide

Padel Score

Padel Score helps you follow a padel match from your watch without interrupting play.

Basic use

  • Open the app before the match starts.
  • Select or confirm the match format if the app offers this setting.
  • Use the on-screen controls to add points during the match.
  • Check the current game, set, and match score on the main screen.

Troubleshooting

  • If the score looks wrong, use the correction or reset option available in the app.
  • If the display does not refresh, close and reopen the app.
  • For support, include your watch model and the app version.

Privacy

Padel Score is designed to work without advertising or tracking. See the privacy policy.
