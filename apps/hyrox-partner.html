App guide

Hyrox Partner

Hyrox Partner supports structured training and race-style sessions from the wrist.

Basic use

  • Open the app before starting your workout or race simulation.
  • Select the available workout, station, or timer mode if provided.
  • Use the app to follow time, transitions, or session progress.
  • Review or reset the session when the workout is complete.

Troubleshooting

  • Check permissions if the app uses workout, fitness, or sensor features.
  • Restart the app if a timer or session state does not update.
  • Contact support with your watch model and a description of the workout mode used.

Privacy

Hyrox Partner is designed to work without advertising or tracking. See the privacy policy.
