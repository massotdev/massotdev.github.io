Contact

If this email is not active yet, use massotdynamics@gmail.com during the domain transition.

Before contacting support

  • Make sure your watch and phone are running the latest available system version.
  • Install the latest version of the app from the App Store or Garmin Connect IQ Store.
  • Restart the app if the score, session, or timer does not update as expected.
  • Include the app name, device model, and a short description of the issue in your message.
