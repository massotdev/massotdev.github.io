Privacy Policy

Massot Development apps are designed without advertising or tracking.

Last updated: May 14, 2026.

Overview

Massot Development does not sell personal data and does not use third-party advertising or tracking services in its apps.

Data collection

Unless clearly stated in a specific app, Massot Development apps do not create user accounts and do not collect personal information on Massot Development servers.

Data stored on your device

Some apps may store preferences, scores, timers, sessions, or settings locally on your device to provide their core features. This local data remains on your device unless you choose to delete the app or your system synchronizes it through platform features controlled by Apple, Garmin, or your device provider.

Health, fitness, and watch permissions

If an app requests access to health, fitness, workout, sensor, or notification permissions, that access is used only to provide the app feature you selected. Massot Development does not sell or use this information for advertising.

Analytics

Massot Development apps do not use third-party analytics by default. If analytics are added to a future app version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

Massot Development apps are not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@massotdev.com.
